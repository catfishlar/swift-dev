
## Integrate with XCode

### OpenCode

In Xcode settings you click Intelligence (macos 27.2.1 on M3)  And "Add an Agent".
- Name: OpenCode
- Executable `/opt/homebrew/bin/opencode`
- arguments:  acp 

Save. 

### Chat

In Xcode Settings click intelligence and below where you "Add an Agent" you can "Add 
a Chat Provider"
- Local Hosted
- Port: 8000
- Description:  Local Mtplx
